Guten Tag! — A full A1 course

Class notes built to help you speak naturally, not just pass a test

Twelve chapters, each with full grammar explanations, sentence and question factories, speaking practice, vocabulary, exercises with an answer key, pronunciation, common mistakes, and a quick revision summary — built to prepare you for real conversation, not just recognition.

Each chapter builds on the last — cases (accusative, dative, two-way prepositions), the Perfekt past tense, and the question-word system are introduced gradually and cross-referenced. If you're starting fresh, work through them in order.
